# Patient-access and communication boundaries

The Cloudflare Worker verifies Firebase signatures, project, issuer, expiry and the strict `email_verified === true` claim. Apps Script accepts identity only through its signed envelope. Browser-supplied role, patient ownership, verification status, approval identity and message body cannot grant authority.

- Staff actions still require an active, approved Users record. Patient and caregiver links are not staff roles. Only administrators/clinicians can grant/revoke patient access after an explicit identity/consent attestation.
- `portal.me`, `portal.passport`, and `portal.request` are the complete patient endpoint set. Every request requires a verified email and an active current-workspace link to that patient. A pending email approval binds to one authenticated UID. A different UID cannot reclaim an already-bound link.
- Portal responses allowlist patient-facing fields. Internal notes, staff conversation, unapproved dose decisions and unreviewed clinical results are not exposed. Requests/replies are scoped to the submitting account as well as the patient.
- Patient INR submissions are unverified, attributed and idempotent. They never insert clinical INR/dose rows. Clinical verification requires an authenticated clinician and a matching patient/date/INR encounter with an actual approval audit. Inaccurate reports can receive a documented clinician correction request; they are not falsely marked verified.
- Message templates use server-selected approved schedules, target snapshots, instructions, dates and recipient preferences. The latest completed encounter must be approved before a care-plan message is generated. Arabic clinical free text must be approved in Arabic rather than automatically translated. Standard text is bilingual, with isolated numeric ranges for RTL readability.
- Drafts belong to the creating staff member, expire after 15 minutes, and are rechecked before opening WhatsApp and recording an outcome. Changes to a plan, target, recipient, WhatsApp consent, or new unverified INR prevent opening a stale routine draft. Failed/not-sent historical attempts can still be recorded; an outdated manually-sent report requires an explicit historical confirmation and note. Such records are flagged, retain the original draft and plan, never claim confirmed delivery, and never close follow-up automatically.
- Audit events record actual identities for access, submissions, reads, replies, verification, preferences, drafts, outcomes and clinical approvals. No fixture creates a clinician signature. Message_Drafts, Communications and Patient_Replies are append-only through the API.
- Additive Patients/Encounters columns and Patient_Access, Patient_Requests, Patient_Replies, Message_Drafts, Communications and Clinic_Settings tabs preserve the existing database. Development reset scopes links and patient communication records out of the active workspace while retaining history, staff accounts and global clinic settings.

The staff workspace batches its initial patient reads, reading each source once. The patient portal polls only while visible and preserves in-memory requests across refresh/language changes. Tokens and health records are not persisted to browser storage; only interface-language preferences are stored there.

No WhatsApp Business API, background auto-send, PHC system integration, report-image/file storage, or guaranteed push-notification service is introduced. Existing Firebase email/password authentication and Worker settings are reused; no service-account key is required. This remains a synthetic-data development application pending clinical validation before live patient use.

Routine portal reads for an already-bound account do not take the clinical mutation lock. Initial account binding is serialized and auditable; patient writes recheck active access inside the mutation lock. Staff workspace responses batch the same authorized fields previously loaded individually, using each source once. A new unverified INR blocks routine care-plan messaging until verification; concerning patient-reported readings have a separate triage warning rather than being treated as clinical measurements.
